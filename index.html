FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

Is ONE BANK a licensed bank?

No. ONE BANK Cooperative is a community cooperative, not a licensed bank. We do not take deposits. You pay for a membership plan tied to a product + referral system.

When can I withdraw?

When your available balance reaches ₦2,000. Request in your dashboard, choose your bank, Admin verifies and pays to your bank.

Do I earn without selling?

No. You earn only when a product is sold or a member joins through your link. No guaranteed profit.

Does membership expire?

No. Membership never expires. No monthly fee. First sale in first 7 days = +₦500 bonus.

How do I order a gadget?

Tap Order via Email or Google Chat on any gadget. Send your name, product, and delivery area. NO WhatsApp. Email: onebankcommunity@gmail.com
